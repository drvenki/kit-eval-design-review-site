FFPE 25 ng carries no pairwise claim

The omnibus is significant at p = 0.032, but no contrast survives correction. KAPAON has the highest mean (0.124) and the ordering matches FFPE 150 ng, yet the batch variance is too large to support a pairwise statement. Report it as "consistent in direction with the 150 ng panel but not individually significant", and resist quoting the uncorrected p-values.

Suggested Table 1 for the manuscript

The journal allows six main figures and tables combined, and the current plan is five figures with no table. Table 1 should be the design table: kit × sample type × number of batches × number of libraries × operator × prep dates × PCR cycles × adapter:insert ratio. It answers most of the review's missing-data list in one object, and it is exactly what your supervisor is asking to have written down. The raw material is on the experimental design page and in manuscript/data/design_layout.tsv.

The per-kit means, SDs, n, ICCs and contrast p-values above then go into a supplementary table rather than competing for a main-text slot.
